title: All Components
---

spawn-ui is taking in every component spawn's screens use. This is stage one (foundations, actions, status). Next come containers (cards, menus, dialogs), then spawn's own session, game and chat components.

## Foundations

- [Icon](./foundations/icon/) — A Phosphor icon at spawn's one weight and three sizes.

## Actions

- [Button](./actions/button/) — A button with words. Never yellow.
- [IconButton](./actions/icon-button/) — A button with only an icon. Its name is said, and shown on hover.
- [SegmentedControl](./actions/segmented-control/) — One choice out of a few, side by side. The chosen one gets a faint fill and bright words.
- [Switch](./actions/switch/) — Turns a setting on or off at once. A setting turned on is a chosen thing, so it is yellow.
- [Checkbox](./actions/checkbox/) — When several may be chosen together, as in a question card's multi-select.
- [RadioGroup](./actions/radio-group/) — One out of a list, as in a question card's options.

## Status

- [StatusDot](./status/status-dot/) — A 6px dot for a state or a tier, never alone: words come with it.
- [Tag](./status/tag/) — A small label in a hairline, never filled.
- [Pill](./status/pill/) — A session's state as a dot and a word.
- [Badge](./status/badge/) — A round badge on the reward yellow: a new Pokémon, a count.
- [Meter](./status/meter/) — A thin bar: context (HP), usage limits, a Pokémon's stat.

## Containers

- [Card](./containers/card/) — A box that holds things: the widget card, the chat card, the well under code and diffs.
- [Notice](./containers/notice/) — A quiet line: a status, a hint, a day, a warning. It wraps; it never hides its words.
- [EmptyState](./containers/empty-state/) — What a place shows while it has nothing yet, or is loading it.
- [Banner](./containers/banner/) — Something that needs the person: always the amber hairline, never yellow.
- [ListRow](./containers/list-row/) — One row of a list: a setting with its control, or an item to pick.
- [Toolbar](./containers/toolbar/) — The bar of filters over a page: a bottom hairline, wrapping when narrow.
- [TextInput](./containers/text-input/) — spawn's text fields. Focus shows by the border turning lighter.
- [SearchField](./containers/search-field/) — A search box over a list or a grid.
- [FindBar](./containers/find-bar/) — Find in a conversation or a terminal, floating at the top right.
- [Dialog](./containers/dialog/) — A window over the app: Tab stays inside, Esc or the backdrop closes it, and the focus goes back on close.
- [Menu](./containers/menu/) — Choices that open from a button: arrows move, Enter picks, Esc closes.
- [Tooltip](./containers/tooltip/) — A short note on hover or keyboard focus. spawn keeps the browser's title until asked.
- [Tabs](./containers/tabs/) — Tabs: arrows move between them; the chosen one shows its panel.
