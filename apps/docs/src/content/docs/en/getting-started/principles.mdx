---
title: Design Principles
description: The rules spawn kept as tests, and why
---

Every rule below is a test. A component that breaks one fails the build.

## Yellow is rare

Yellow (`--accent`) marks **what is chosen** and nothing else: the chosen session, the picked cell, send, the update button, a switch turned on. Even when something seems to need emphasis, yellow is not added: it is rare so that the chosen thing stands out at a glance. The strong action button (`Button variant="solid"`) is ink, not yellow.

## Three greys

Text comes in three greys only: `--ink` (body), `--ink-2` (secondary), `--ink-3` (quiet).

- Body text is **4.5:1 or more** on every surface; secondary and quiet text **3:1 or more**.
- Tier colours are 3:1 or more on every surface too.

## Never colour alone

A state is said by a dot and a word together. A tier is always its dot beside its name, never a chip filled with the tier's colour. Status dots carry text for screen readers.

## One focus ring

Only keyboard focus shows a ring: `2px solid var(--ink-2)`. None on a click, never yellow. Rows and menu items, which sit flush with their neighbours, draw it inside (-2px).

## Weights and radii

- Weights are 380 (code), 400 (body), 500 (names) and 600 (headings) only.
- Radii are the `--r-*` tokens, 0, 1px, 2px and 50 % only. Fully round (999px) is for the switch and badges alone.

## Grow with the text size

spawn's text size goes from 80 % to 130 %, so components never set font sizes in px: only the `--t-*` tokens. Try it with the 80–130 % buttons above every preview.

## Motion: short and quiet

Transitions take 120–180ms on one easing, `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`. When the system asks for less motion, every transition and animation stops. Nothing glints or spins to say it is working.
